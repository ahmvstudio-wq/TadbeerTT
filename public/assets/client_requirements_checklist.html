Client Data Collection Requirements

Information & Data Collection Checklist

To customize the Human Capital Transformation System, design tailor-made SOPs, and configure the digital management dashboard for your retail group, Tadbeer requests the following organizational data:

Category 01
Store Network & Showroom Locations
Pilot Showroom Location: Exact address & layout details of the flagship showroom selected for Phase 1 pilot.
Branch Locations List: Addresses and store sizes of all remaining showroom branches across Oman and Dubai.
Store Headcount: Number of frontline sales staff, cashiers, supervisors, and store managers per showroom location.
Category 02
Employee Profiles & Roster Data
Complete Staff Roster: List of current store employees with Job Titles, Designations & Department.
Employee Demographics: Age, nationality, years of service, and language capabilities.
Role Responsibility Mapping: Current assigned daily duties per job title (Sales, Stocking, Cashiering, Supervision).
Category 03
Current Operations & SOP Manuals
Company Policy Manual: Existing Employee Handbook, Code of Conduct & HR policies.
Current SOP Documents: Store opening/closing checklists, product handling, grooming & customer service guidelines.
Attendance & Shift Tracking System: Details on current time-attendance software or manual roster tracking.
Category 04
Compensation & HR Benefits Structure
Salary & Commission Structure: Base salary bands, target commissions, and performance bonus policies.
Leave & Flight Allowance: Annual leave entitlement, air ticket allowance rules & gratuity payout terms (e.g. 2-year payout cycle).
Medical & Secondary Benefits: Employer medical insurance coverage levels for employees and dependents.
Category 05: Organizational Hierarchy & Approval Workflows
Current Org Chart: Reporting lines from frontline staff up to Store Managers, Regional Managers, and C-Suite.
Approval Workflows: Escalation paths for discount approvals, customer complaints, stock returns, and schedule swaps.
