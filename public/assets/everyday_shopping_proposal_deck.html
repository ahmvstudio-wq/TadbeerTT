Common Challenges: Why Retailers Stop Growing

Every Day Shopping Center generates strong footfall and revenue (264K OMR in Q2 2026), but faces 4 critical blockers to scaling online.

Challenge 01
Social Algorithm Dependency & Loose Funnel
Relying 100% on Instagram views (@every.d24) without an owned customer list or direct CRM. Followers must DM and wait, causing massive customer drop-off.
No automated link from Reel interest to checkout catalog
No direct subscriber capture to buffer against reach drops
High customer friction during manual DM-to-purchase flows
Risk: Organic reach fluctuations can instantly hurt footfall
Challenge 02
Empty E-Commerce Storefront (Udu)
Udu e-commerce account exists but remains completely unstocked, unbranded, and unpromoted. High-value digital traffic is directed only to a map link.
Paying for e-commerce tools with 0 SKUs listed online
No capability to accept 24/7 web orders from Qurum & Muscat
Storefront lacks visual branding, trust badges, or local payment options
Risk: Wasted software budgets & missed out-of-store sales
Challenge 03
Unstructured POS Product Data
Our POS data audit shows 96-97% of sales categorized under "Not Categorized" or "TEMP" buckets. The product data cannot be synced to Udu cleanly as-is.
15,000+ SKUs lack clean hierarchy or systemized tags
Manual listing is too slow; bulk import requires database cleanup
High risk of inventory mismatches between physical store and online stock
Risk: Inventory sync blockers prevent e-commerce launch
Challenge 04
Unstructured Visual Branding & Competition
Staff-hosted Reels perform well but lack consistent templates or identity. New rivals (e.g. C Flash nearby) capture market share by copying discount offers.
No highlight covers, style guides, or post templates
Brand appears as a discount-only seller rather than a reliable center
Lacks consistent video formats to convert viral views into buyers
Risk: Price wars with local copycats erode margins
