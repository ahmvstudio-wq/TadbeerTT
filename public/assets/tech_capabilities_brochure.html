7 Core Problem Domains & High-Leverage AI Solutions

We attack the 7 most painful operational bottlenecks in Omani urban retail with specialized AI agents wired into cloud data pipelines.

1 POS, Inventory & Stock Control
Pain: Peak period billing delays, inaccurate inventory, intuitive over-stocking, dead stock, and cash locked in slow-moving SKUs.
Cloud POS Spine: Arabic/English, offline-tolerant, integrated with local receipt printers & payment terminals.
AI Inventory Employee: Virtual stock controller that monitors sell-through, generates automated purchase orders, and sends daily WhatsApp digests to owners.
2 Omni-Channel & Social Commerce
Pain: Disconnected WhatsApp/Instagram sales, double-sold stock, price inconsistencies across physical & digital storefronts.
RetailOps Router: Single order hub unifying POS, Shopify/Woo, WhatsApp & Instagram DMs into one pipeline.
AI Sales Assistant: Chat agents on WhatsApp/Instagram that verify live inventory, take orders, process payments, and push delivery tasks.
3 Customer Data & Loyalty
Pain: Customer data trapped in POS receipts, zero personalized marketing, reliance on manual discount spams.
Micro-CDP & AI Segmentation: Ingests receipt & chat data into a single customer profile, auto-segmenting VIPs & lapsed buyers.
AI Marketing Employee: Generates targeted WhatsApp/SMS broadcasts and manages app-free punch-card loyalty logic.
4 Owner Visibility & Decision Support
Pain: Store owners juggling bank statements & gut feel; unable to calculate true margins or expansion readiness.
Founder Cockpit: Real-time visual dashboards showing gross margins, cash cycle, and channel performance.
Conversational AI CFO: On-demand WhatsApp chat assistant that answers complex business questions and simulates financial scenarios.
