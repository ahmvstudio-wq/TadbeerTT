Common Challenges: Why Retailers Stop Growing

Many fragrance brands have great products but struggle with operations that slow down their sales and lower their profits.

Bottleneck 01
Unowned Social Storefronts
Relying 100% on manual Instagram DMs and phone calls without an owned e-commerce website or centralized customer database.
No structured catalog with SKUs, pricing, or reviews
High customer drop-off due to manual DM delays
Founder tied down in repetitive manual product description links
Risk: Total reliance on social algorithms & lost sales leads
Bottleneck 02
Prestige Erosion via Heavy Discounts
Depending constantly on "50% Off" discount promotions to drive sales, eroding brand value and sacrificing profit margins.
Commoditizing luxury perfumes into discount offers
Slashing profit margins on international brand decants
Training customers to only purchase when discounts are active
Risk: Low profit margins & training buyers to wait for sales
Bottleneck 03
Zero Customer Retention Systems
Lacking an automated CRM or loyalty program to re-engage buyers for Eid, National Day, birthdays, and seasonal fragrance gifting.
No record of customer scent preferences or past purchases
Zero automated WhatsApp broadcasts for seasonal gifting
High customer churn rates after first perfume purchase completes
Risk: High customer acquisition costs & zero repeat LTV
Bottleneck 04
Manual Order & Inventory Friction
Handling orders, decant pricing, and stock tracking manually on paper or chat, causing billing delays and inventory mismatches.
Stockouts on fast-selling perfume 10L decant stock
Manual invoice creation & payment tracking delays
Errors in customer shipping details during chat transcription
Risk: Stockouts, lost orders & staff processing bottlenecks
